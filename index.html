The Problem

A future without fair coordination is a future we can't afford

Privatization Risk

Critical AI infrastructure is being captured by a few corporations, locking out individual contributors and communities from the value they help create.

Tragedy of the Commons

Without shared governance standards, open systems risk exploitation — where everyone takes but nobody sustains, leaving critical infrastructure fragile.

Contributor Exclusion

Contributors — developers, researchers, community builders, and community organizations — lack the economic and social infrastructure to sustain their vital work.

Our Approach

Shared standards and stewardship for the agentic era

We are creating a shared standards and stewardship layer rooted in FOJ principles across all interaction types — enabling communities to collectively govern critical infrastructure, reward contributors, and build futures that are neither overly privatized nor vulnerable to collapse.

Core Principles

What FOJ stands for

F

Fair

Economic models that reward contributors equitably. No one should sustain critical infrastructure without viable compensation. We make contributions economically and socially viable for individuals.

O

Open

Transparent governance, open standards, and shared infrastructure. Communities should collectively own and steer the systems they depend on — not be locked into proprietary platforms.

J

Justice

Systems designed for inclusion from the ground up. Every stakeholder — from individual developers to entire communities — deserves a voice in how AI-human coordination is governed.

S

Sustainable

We design systems to support long-term resilience so creators and creations, communities and societies flourish — not just survive, but thrive across generations.

Where We Work
